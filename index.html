California DMV Planned Non-Operation (PNO) Strategy

Paying full California DMV registration fees for 5 stored/non-operational vehicles costs ~$915.00. By filing Planned Non-Operation (PNO) on the CA DMV website (dmv.ca.gov) or by phone (800-777-0133), the cost is only ~$25.00 per vehicle (approx $125 total), immediately saving ~$790.00 and halting late fee accumulation!

⚠️ Note: When selling, Autumn (as POA) will sign the title transfer (DMV Form REG 227 / Bill of Sale).
